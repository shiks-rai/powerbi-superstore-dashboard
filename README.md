# Superstore Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

Interactive 2-page Power BI dashboard analysing retail sales, profit, products and regions (2014-2017).

![Overview](images/page1-overview.png)
![Products](images/page2-products.png)

## What it shows
- KPIs: total sales, profit, orders, customers, profit margin
- Monthly sales trend, sales and profit by category and region
- Product-level profit by sub-category and sales vs profit scatter
- Slicers for category, region and year
